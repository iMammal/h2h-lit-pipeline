# Title/abstract screening fast-track amendment 2.1.0

Approved prospectively on 2026-09-22 for staging-only, time-bounded use. This amendment does not reinterpret returns completed under protocol 2.0.0 and does not modify production screening state, registered evidence, identification closure, or PRISMA.

## Scientific outcome

E1–E5 are unchanged. A defensible scientific `NO` produces `EXCLUDED` with an evidence-supported reason. With no E1–E5 `NO`, unresolved scientific evidence produces `UNCERTAIN`. `INCLUDE` requires `YES` for E1–E5 and sufficient title/abstract evidence under E7.

E7 remains an evidence-sufficiency criterion, not a topical eligibility criterion. `NO` or `UNCERTAIN` on E7 prevents `INCLUDE`; it does not convert missing evidence into a scientific exclusion.

E6 remains `NOT_ASSESSED_AT_THIS_STAGE`. Administrative verification occurs during full-report assessment, and final inclusion requires both scientific eligibility and E6 verification.

## Operational disposition

Scientific outcome and workflow disposition are separate fields:

- `EXCLUDE`: a defensible E1–E5 failure supports exclusion.
- `ADVANCE_TO_FULL_REPORT_ASSESSMENT`: the title/abstract evidence clearly supports E1–E5 and E7, and a full report is verified available.
- `DEFER`: evidence is insufficient, the abstract is missing, evidence is conflicted, or the full report is unavailable in this round.

`DEFER` is not an exclusion criterion and does not establish final eligibility. Deferred records retain their reason and available evidence. They may be revisited only for a documented synthesis gap, never to reach a target paper count.

## Reporting and limitations

Each run reports the actual counts screened, excluded, advanced, deferred, and still unprocessed. This is pragmatic, time-bounded selection subject to missing-evidence and accessibility limitations. It is not exhaustive screening, does not establish unbiased coverage, and does not turn this exposed protocol-development pilot into independent validation.
